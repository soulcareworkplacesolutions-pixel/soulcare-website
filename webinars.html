Common Questions
Are SoulCare webinars free?
Most of our public webinars are free. Structured programmes like Thrive at Work are priced per company. Check each listing for details.
Can I bring my whole team?
Yes — you are welcome to share the link with your colleagues. Wellness works best when teams experience it together.
How do I receive the Google Meet link?
After registration, the Meet link is sent to your email. We also send a WhatsApp reminder before the session begins.
Can SoulCare run a private session for our company?
Yes. We deliver private corporate webinars on ergonomics, sleep, nutrition, and mindfulness — customised to your team. Write to hello@soulcareworkplace.com with your company name and team size.
What makes Thrive at Work different from a regular webinar?
Every Thrive at Work session is a live guided experience — not a presentation. Employees move, adjust, breathe, and act during the session itself. WPI scores are measured before and after so you have proof of the change.
