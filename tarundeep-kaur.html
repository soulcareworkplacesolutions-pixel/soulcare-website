About Tarundeep Kaur

Tarundeep Kaur (Daisy to her participants) is Head, Wellness Operations, Growth and Client Success at SoulCare™ Workplace Solutions, where she designs and leads its facilitator-led programmes across India. She brings over a decade of experience delivering wellness sessions to professionals and teams.

Her sessions draw on psychology, mindfulness, therapeutic art and coaching methods. The aim is practical: managers and teams who handle pressure better, communicate more clearly and carry themselves with confidence.

Measurement

How Results Are Measured

Every standalone workshop includes a short pulse survey before and after the session. It covers perceived stress, emotional regulation and team communication. Your HR team receives an anonymised summary within 7 working days.

When a programme runs as part of a SoulCare™ Workplace Performance Programme, results are tracked with the Workplace Performance Index (WPI). The WPI is SoulCare™'s own index, scored out of 100 across four pillars: Clinical and Pain Management; Movement and Performance; Mental Wellness and Stress; Nutrition and Lifestyle. It is measured at baseline, monitored during the programme and reassessed at completion. We share the questions with your HR team before the programme starts.
