About SoulCare
The problem isn't wellness spend.
It's unmeasured wellness spend.

Most Indian employers spend meaningfully on employee wellness — health camps, insurance top-ups, occasional yoga sessions, one-off webinars. Almost none can prove what that spend actually changed.

SoulCare Workplace Solutions was built on one observation: corporate wellness in India is activity-driven, not outcome-driven. We built a system that changes that.

Every SoulCare engagement produces a Workplace Wellbeing Index (WPI) score at baseline, monthly, and conclusion — along with a quantified Return on Investment figure in rupees. Not participation rates. Not survey satisfaction. Actual measurable health outcomes tied to actual business value.

We are a private limited company headquartered in Guwahati, Assam, with operations in Bengaluru and expanding across India.

Why measurement changes everything

When wellness is measured, it stops being a cost centre and starts being an investment. The HR head can defend it. The CFO can verify it. The employee can feel it. And the renewal rate reflects it.

The Framework
Four pillars. One system.
Every programme is built on these four pillars. Clients can buy one or all four. The framework is what makes outcomes comparable.

Clinical & Pain Management

PILLAR 02

Movement & Performance

Mobility drills, desk-break routines, strength training, fitness activation. Reversing sedentary damage.

PILLAR 03

Mental Wellness & Stress

Stress workshops, breathwork, mindfulness practices, focus training. Addressing the burnout epidemic.

PILLAR 04

Lifestyle & Preventive Health

Nutrition guidance, sleep education, habit-building, preventive care. The daily choices that compound.
