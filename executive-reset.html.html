The Invitation

Sometimes, you just need to step away.

You spend your days looking after things. Your business. Your team. Your clients. Your people. There is always another call to take, another problem to solve, another decision waiting.

Looking after yourself keeps getting pushed to "later". Executive Reset is for that "later".

It is not another wellness workshop, and nobody will tell you how to live. It is one day to slow down, breathe, think, move, and spend time with people who understand the pressure you work under.

Why Executive Reset

Stress does not always look like stress.

Sometimes it looks like this:

A mind that will not switch off

Even when the laptop is closed.

Tired after a full night's sleep

Rest that never quite restores you.

No moment to breathe

One meeting straight into the next.

Always being available

Every message answered, every time.

Carrying work home

The day never fully ends.

Never finding the day

You know you need a break. The right week never comes.

Executive Reset is not about walking away from your responsibilities. It is about stepping back from them long enough to see where you stand, think about what you need, and return with a little more clarity.

The Experience

A day away from the noise.

A small, carefully planned experience built around stress, reflection, recovery and real conversation. The pace is deliberate. There is room to take part, room to stay quiet, and room to do nothing at all.

4 December | Arrive. Unwind. Settle in.
6:00 PM onward

Arrival and Check-in

Arrive at the retreat venue, check in and settle into your room.

7:00 PM

Welcome and Opening

Meet the SoulCare™ team and the small group you will spend tomorrow with. Nothing formal. The Executive Reset begins.

7:30 PM

Dinner

A relaxed dinner and easy conversation. No presentations. No business agenda.

8:30 PM

The Unfinished Day

A guided personal reflection on the day behind you: what is still open, and what you can set down before you sleep.

9:15 PM

Slow Stretch Session

Fifteen minutes of slow, gentle stretching to let go of the day before you sleep.

9:30 PM

Quiet Close

Personal time and rest. You stay overnight at the retreat venue.

5 December | Your Executive Reset
6:00 AM

Nature Walk and Guided Reflection

A quiet walk with simple questions to notice what has been on your mind. What has been taking most of my energy? What have I been carrying for too long? What needs my attention now?

7:15 AM

Herbal Tea and Light Food

A gentle start to the day, with nutrition support from Dr. Srishti.

7:45 AM

Executive Mobility Reset

Easy mobility work for the neck, shoulders, back and hips, to undo the stiffness of long days at a desk and on the road.

8:15 AM

Shower and Personal Reset Break

Time to freshen up, rest and reset before breakfast.

9:00 AM

Breakfast

A proper breakfast and unhurried time with the group, with nutrition support from Dr. Srishti.

10:00 AM

Visual Reflection Lab: When Colors Speak

Some things are easier to express than to explain. A guided stress-management experience using colour to explore thoughts and feelings that usually stay in the background. You do not need to know how to paint.

12:00 PM

Immersive Mindful Movement

Slow, flowing movement at your own pace to settle the body before lunch. Nothing competitive. Nothing to prove.

1:00 PM

Lunch

A relaxed meal and time to recharge.

2:00 PM

Leadership Reflection Lab

A guided reflection on how you lead, what it asks of you, and what you want to change, with guided pair exercises.

3:30 PM

Guided Journaling: My 30-Day Reset

Write down what you want to take from the day and shape it into a personal 30-day plan.

4:30 PM

Tea and Connection

An easy pause. Carry on a conversation, meet someone new, or simply enjoy the surroundings.

5:00 PM

Conversations That Matter

Honest conversation in small groups, built around a few good questions. Nobody is made to share. Just good listening and fresh perspective.

6:15 PM

Gratitude and Closing Circle

The day closes with a circle of gratitude. Look back at what stood out and decide what you want to carry forward. No big promises. Just one or two things that matter to you.

6:45 PM

Departure

Safe travels. Your 30-Day SoulCare™ Reset continues from here.
